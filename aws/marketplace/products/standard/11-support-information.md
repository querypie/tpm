## English - AWS source

QueryPie ACP Standard Edition includes online documentation and community-based support through QueryPie Community Discussions.
Support is provided on a best-effort basis with no response-time SLA.
For AWS Marketplace subscription, billing, or refund requests, contact [MARKETPLACE_SUPPORT_EMAIL].
AWS infrastructure charges are billed separately and are outside QueryPie's software refund policy.
